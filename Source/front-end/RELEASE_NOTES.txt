NHL is here: game picks, player projections, standings and the playoff bracket. New-look game, golf and race screens show each pick as a betting line and how it turned out, with simpler filters.
Your browser and phone can now save and fill in your sign-in details.
Games now flag upsets: UPSET WATCH when our model thinks the betting favorite could lose, UPSET when they did, plus a new Upsets filter.
Playoff brackets now show a realistic run with each team's chances, series wins and upset flags, plus every team's odds of reaching each round, updated daily during the playoffs.
Player predictions now include stars who changed teams, and rookies are no longer judged on their college stats.
Fixed a sign-in problem that could leave the app showing errors after a while away. Finished games now show their final score without a refresh.
The Raiders' team color is now silver.
